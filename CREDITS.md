## Licence summary

- **CC BY-SA** — attribution required, and modifications of the *image* must be
  shared alike. Displaying an image in an application does not make the
  application a derivative work.
- **CC BY** — attribution required.
- **CC0 / Public domain** — no obligation; credited here where an author is known.

Photographs taken by users inside the app never leave their browser and are not
covered by any of the above.
